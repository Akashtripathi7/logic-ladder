Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

In short: Logic Ladder uses Google sign-in only to save your learning progress to your own account. We store your name, email address, profile photo and which lessons and problems you have completed. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone except the services that run the site.

Who we are

Logic Ladder (logic-ladder.vercel.app) is a free course for learning logic, Python and data structures and algorithms. It is run by Akash Tripathi. The source code is public on GitHub.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, Google shares the following with us. We ask for nothing else from your Google account:

  • Your name and profile photo, shown in the top bar while you are signed in.
  • Your email address and a Google account ID, which identify your account so your progress is saved to the right place.

While you use the course, we save:

  • Your progress: which modules you mark complete and which problems you mark solved.
  • A history of those actions, each with the time it happened, so your progress is accurate across devices.

We do not collect your Google contacts, files, calendar or any other Google data, and we do not read the code you write.

What stays in your browser

Some settings are stored only in your own browser (local storage) and never sent to us: your language (English or Hinglish), light or dark theme, how far you have watched each lesson video, your best score in the mini ladder game, and a local copy of your progress so pages load quickly. Lesson narration uses your browser's built-in speech voice.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To save your progress and show it to you on any device you use.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or marketing emails, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Services that process your data

We rely on these providers to run the site. Each only receives what it needs to do its job:

  • Google: provides "Sign in with Google".
  • Supabase: stores your account and progress (authentication and database).
  • Vercel: hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: deliver the site's fonts and a code library. Like any website, they see your IP address when your browser loads these files.

How your data is protected

All traffic uses HTTPS. In the database, access rules mean a signed-in user can only read and add to their own progress. No one else using the site can see it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and progress while your account exists, so your progress is there when you come back. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and all of your progress history.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: ask us and we will delete your account and progress. See the contact section below.
  • Remove access: you can remove Logic Ladder's access at any time from your Google account, under Third-party connections.
  • Sign out: signing out clears this browser's copy of your progress.

Children

Logic Ladder is meant for learners aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

For questions about your data or to ask for it to be deleted, contact us through the Logic Ladder GitHub page. Please don't post your email address publicly; say you'd like your data deleted and we will arrange a private way to confirm it is you.
